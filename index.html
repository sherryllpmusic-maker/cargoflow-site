    Questions people ask

    Quick answers

    What is CargoFlow?

    Transport, fleet and warehouse management software made for India. It brings a fleet owner’s trucks, a godown’s stock and the money between them into one place.

    Who is it for?

    Fleet owners and transporters, warehouse and godown operators, and the accounts and operations teams who work for them.

    Is it only for trucking?

    No. Trucking and warehousing together, including floor-stacked godowns, racked stores and liquid or tanker cargo.

    Does it work on a phone?

    Yes. It runs in the browser on an ordinary phone, in English and Hindi.

    Does it handle GST, TDS and Indian paperwork?

    It is built around them: GST invoicing, TDS, FASTag tolls, e-way bills and challans, so your accountant sees books they recognise.

    Can it replace our Excel sheets and WhatsApp groups?

    That is the aim. Reports can still go to Excel or PDF whenever someone needs them.

    How do I get a demo?

    Tap Book a demo, pick who you are, what hurts most and a time. No form to fill.
